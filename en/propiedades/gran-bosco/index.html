Current promotion

November is Buen Fin at Gran Bosco! Exclusive offer for phase 1 homes: 1 year of free maintenance fees and 2 inverter mini-split air conditioners (12,000 BTU). Ask us which homes qualify. Valid through November 30, 2026.

Description

A gated community of 352 homes on the road to Chicxulub, in Conkal, with more than 10,000 m² of amenities: resort-style pool, clubhouse with gym and event room, padel courts, soccer field, jogging track and pet park.

There are 15 models to choose from, from 104 to 203 m² of construction, on lots of 9 to 11 m of frontage. Every home is delivered with a pool, fitted kitchen with upper and lower cabinets, tempered-glass shower panels and a covered or semi-covered carport.

Good to know: the project does not allow changes or modifications to the homes. The estimated maintenance fee is $2,300 MXN per month (70% in phase 1 and 80% in phase 2 while amenities are delivered in stages), plus an $8,000 MXN reserve fund paid at closing.

Schedule a visit and we’ll help you choose the model and lot that best fit you.
