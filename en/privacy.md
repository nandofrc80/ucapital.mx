We only collect the data you voluntarily provide through our forms or contact channels: name, WhatsApp number, email address, budget range, type of property of interest, delivery preference and purpose (investment or residence), as well as any additional comments you choose to share.

## 3. Purposes of processing

Your personal data will be used for the following primary purposes, which are necessary for our relationship with you:

- Responding to your request for information and following up on it.
- Presenting property options that match your criteria.
- Coordinating visits, calls and meetings.
- Supporting you through negotiation, signing and closing.
- Complying with applicable legal and tax obligations.

As a secondary purpose, and with your prior authorization, we may send you information about new developments, investment opportunities and other communications of interest. You may object to these secondary purposes at any time without affecting the handling of your main request.

U·Capital does not sell or commercialize your personal data. We may share it only with developers, notaries, financial institutions or technical advisors when strictly necessary to complete the transaction you requested, and under the corresponding confidentiality commitment. Any other transfer will require your express consent.

## 5. ARCO rights

You have the right to Access, Rectify, Cancel or Object to the processing of your personal data, as well as to revoke any consent given. To exercise any of these rights, you can send your request to contacto@ucapital.mx or via WhatsApp to wa.me/529999562850, stating your full name and the right you wish to exercise. We will respond within twenty business days.

Your data is kept in systems with reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect its confidentiality, integrity and availability, and to prevent unauthorized use, loss, alteration or access.
